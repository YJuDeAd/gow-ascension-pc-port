1. Detects code-end boundary using `./tools/find_code_end.py` (`0x0062C4CC`).

4. Executes `ps3recomp/tools/ppu_lifter.py` with `--code-end 0x0062C4CC`, `--hle-stubs`, `--chunks 8`, and parallel jobs into `./src/gen/`.

```powershell

```
